# Practice corpus content provenance notes

This file documents **content/data** provenance for the Practice corpus. It does not extend or reinterpret the repository's code license, and the build system never infers content-use permission from a repository/SPDX label alone.

## `ws-original-en-foundation-v1`

- Source type: `wordstrike-original`
- Usage approval: `practice-display-approved`
- Recorded content-license metadata: `WordStrike-authored original corpus material`
- SPDX: not asserted
- Upstream: not applicable
- Snapshot checksum: recorded in `sources.json` and verified as SHA-256 during every build/validation
- Scope: tiny PL6 English foundation samples only; not production-complete corpus coverage

Usage approval is an explicit reviewed project record. It is not an automated legal conclusion.

## Legacy gameplay/ranked datasets

Existing gameplay vocabulary and ranked English-200 data remain outside this provenance registry. PL6 does not claim to resolve or broaden the licensing/provenance of those legacy datasets, and they are not Practice corpus sources.
