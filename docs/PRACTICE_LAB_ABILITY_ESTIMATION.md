# Practice Lab PL13 — Ability Estimator + Measurement Uncertainty

Status: PL13 measurement and latent-ability foundation

## 1. Purpose

PL13 separates **session performance** from **estimated underlying typing ability**.

The permanent distinction is:

```text
one test result
≠
stable typing ability
```

Observed performance combines at least:

```text
stable ability
+ temporary session variation
+ text difficulty
+ measurement noise
```

PL13 therefore treats each qualifying completed session as one noisy observation of one context-specific ability channel. It applies a conservative text-difficulty adjustment, estimates observation uncertainty, and recursively updates a slowly changing latent state in log-WPM space.

PL13 does not interpret readiness, warm-up, fatigue, mastery, learning trend, review priority, treatment choice, or Coach value.

## 3. Ability is not a skill stat

Entity evidence remains in `skillStats`:

```text
key
bigram
trigram
word
```

General ability is stored separately because it describes a broader performance channel rather than one entity.

Canonical ability identity is:

```text
profileId
+ contextId
+ channel
```

The deterministic state ID is generated by:

```text
createPracticeAbilityStateId(profileId, contextId, channel)
```

with Practice prefix:

```text
practice-ability_
```

No ability state is copied to another context and PL13 has no cross-context prior.

## 4. Canonical channels

PL13 v1 defines exactly seven channels:

```text
cold-natural-text
controlled-speed
common-words
burst
endurance
punctuation
numbers-symbols
```

There is deliberately no `overall` channel.

A burst result, a cold natural-text result, and an endurance result measure different protocols. PL13 does not average them into a synthetic master WPM.

### cold-natural-text

Unseen/protected natural text without target enrichment. Intended as the eventual general-copy channel.

### controlled-speed

Broadly standardized, accuracy-preserving measurement. It is not PB speed and not targeted-drill speed.

### common-words

High-frequency lexical automaticity under a dedicated common-word protocol. It is not natural-text ability.

### burst

Short-duration upper execution capacity. It never updates another channel.

### endurance

Sustained long-form capability. It remains separate from short cold natural text.

### punctuation

Punctuation/capitalization-heavy practical typing capability.

### numbers-symbols

Practical number/symbol-heavy typing capability.

Domain channels such as code, academic, office, and chat are not present in v1.

## 5. Trusted measurement intent

Practice experiment descriptors have optional:

```text
abilityChannel
```

Default:

```text
null
```

Only a registered trusted descriptor establishes measurement intent. Session configuration containing `abilityChannel` is rejected.

This prevents a targeted drill from declaring itself a cold natural benchmark.

Descriptor intent is necessary but not sufficient. The session must also satisfy the channel's role, targeting, correction, duration, character-volume, accuracy, status, and completion-reason policy.

## 6. Canonical channel policy v1

All thresholds below are versioned engineering values, not scientifically optimal constants.

| Channel | Allowed roles | Duration | Min typed chars | Min accuracy | Untargeted | Correction allowed |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| cold-natural-text | transfer, benchmark | 30–600 s | 100 | 70% | required | required |
| controlled-speed | benchmark, diagnostic | 20–300 s | 75 | 70% | required | required |
| common-words | training, diagnostic | 15–180 s | 50 | 70% | required | required |
| burst | training, diagnostic | 5–15 s | 25 | 70% | required | required |
| endurance | transfer, benchmark | 180–1800 s | 500 | 70% | required | required |
| punctuation | diagnostic, benchmark | 30–300 s | 100 | 70% | required | required |
| numbers-symbols | diagnostic, benchmark | 30–300 s | 100 | 70% | required | required |

Allowed completion reasons are:

```text
time-complete
content-complete
word-target-complete
```

`manual-stop` is rejected for ability measurement to reduce user-selected stopping bias.

Abandoned, interrupted, invalid, navigation, and refresh sessions do not update ability. PL11 may still retain evidence from a meaningful abandoned session; PL13 intentionally uses a stricter measurement contract.

All v1 channels require:

```text
targetEntities.length === 0
correctionBehavior === allow
```

`custom` and `unclassified` content roles do not update ability.

## 7. Measurement assessment

The pure observation builder returns one of:

```text
not-requested
not-eligible
eligible
```

`not-requested` means the trusted descriptor has `abilityChannel: null`.

`not-eligible` means an ability channel was requested but ordinary protocol requirements were not satisfied. This does not invalidate the typing session itself.

Canonical bounded reason codes include:

```text
wrong-session-status
manual-stop
role-not-allowed
targeted-content
correction-policy
duration-too-short
duration-too-long
insufficient-characters
accuracy-too-low
invalid-wpm
context-mismatch
invalid-normalization
```

Ordinary ineligibility is returned as data rather than thrown. Invariant corruption—such as mismatched context identity, unsupported channel, or malformed measurement structure—fails closed.

One completed session produces at most one ability observation and therefore updates exactly one channel or none.

## 8. Performance input

The primary performance input is canonical Practice:

```text
summary.wpm
```

not `rawWpm`.

Current Practice WPM is based on final correct characters over active time, so correction cost naturally remains in the denominator. PL13 does not multiply WPM by accuracy again.

`rawWpm` may be retained in the bounded recent-observation audit record for diagnostics but does not drive the latent update.

An observation is rejected when WPM is nonfinite or `<= 0`.

A valid low-WPM session is not rejected merely for being surprising. Doing that would bias the estimate upward.

## 9. Log-WPM measurement space

The internal measurement variable is:

```math
Y = \ln(WPM)
```

This gives multiplicative changes a consistent additive interpretation and prevents negative ability estimates.

User-facing ability remains:

```math
EstimateWpm = e^\mu
```

Log units are not intended for ordinary UI.

## 10. PL10 difficulty adjustment

PL13 is the first phase that converts PL10 `difficultyIndex` into an observation-level performance adjustment.

Policy v1:

```text
difficultyLogCoefficient = 0.03
maxAbsoluteDifficultyLogAdjustment = 0.12
```

Let:

```text
D = PL10 difficultyIndex
C = availableModelWeight, clamped to 0..1
```

Then:

```math
A_d = clamp(0.03 \times D \times C, -0.12, +0.12)
```

and:

```math
ObservedLogWpm = \ln(WPM)
AdjustedLogPerformance = ObservedLogWpm + A_d
AdjustedWpm = e^{AdjustedLogPerformance}
```

Harder text (`D > 0`) receives a positive ability adjustment. Easier text receives a negative adjustment.

Partial typability coverage scales the correction by `availableModelWeight`; a 50%-covered model therefore receives half the uncapped correction.

For PL10 status:

```text
insufficient
unsupported-language
```

PL13 uses:

```text
difficultyAdjustmentLog = 0
```

and increases observation uncertainty instead of guessing a correction.

PL13 never converts PL10 relative difficulty percentile directly into WPM correction.

This coefficient is a **conservative v1 engineering heuristic**, not an empirically calibrated causal passage-difficulty coefficient. PL18 may later replace or augment the observation adjustment without redesigning `abilityStates`.

## 12. Observation uncertainty

Every eligible observation receives explicit uncertainty in log-WPM space.

### 12.1 Base duration sigma

Reference policy:

```text
baseSigmaLogAt60Seconds = 0.08
```

For ordinary channels:

```math
\sigma_{duration}
= 0.08 \sqrt{\frac{60}{clamp(durationSeconds, 15, 300)}}
```

Burst uses an explicit channel floor of 5 seconds:

```math
\sigma_{duration,burst}
= 0.08 \sqrt{\frac{60}{clamp(durationSeconds, 5, 300)}}
```

Examples before modifiers:

```text
15 s   ≈ 0.16
60 s   = 0.08
240 s  ≈ 0.04
```

### 12.2 Accuracy penalty

Let:

```text
a = accuracy / 100
```

Then:

```math
P_a = 1 + 2.5\max(0, 0.97-a)
```

Examples:

```text
97%+ → 1.000
90%  → 1.175
80%  → 1.425
```

Accuracy is a reliability signal, not another multiplication of performance.

### 12.3 Rhythm penalty

When PL8 supplies positive `fluentMedianMs` and nonnegative `fluentMadMs`:

```math
CV_r = fluentMadMs / fluentMedianMs
```

```math
P_r = 1 + \min(0.5, CV_r)
```

If robust fluency metrics are unavailable:

```text
P_r = 1.15
```

### 12.4 Difficulty-model penalty

```text
full                 1.00
partial              1.15
insufficient         1.35
unsupported-language 1.35
```

### 12.5 Interruption penalty

When PL8 interruption rate is available:

```math
P_i = 1 + \min(0.5, 2 \times interruptionRate)
```

Otherwise:

```text
P_i = 1
```

### 12.6 Trace-quality penalty

For PL8 retained-window coverage:

```text
P_t = 1.10
```

Otherwise:

```text
P_t = 1.00
```

Trace truncation does not invalidate canonical WPM; it only inflates uncertainty where supporting rhythm/interruption evidence is partial.

### 12.7 Final sigma

```math
\sigma
= clamp(
  \sigma_{duration}
  \times P_a
  \times P_r
  \times P_d
  \times P_i
  \times P_t,
  0.04,
  0.30
)
```

Measurement variance:

```math
R = \sigma^2
```

Diagnostic reliability weight:

```math
ReliabilityWeight
= clamp((0.08/\sigma)^2, 0.05, 4)
```

The variance remains the estimator input; `reliabilityWeight` does not replace it.

An observation is counted as downweighted when:

```text
measurementSigmaLog > 0.12
```

## 13. Recursive latent estimator

PL13 uses a scalar Kalman-style recursive estimator in log-WPM space. It has no external Bayesian dependency.

Policy v1:

```text
initialSigmaLog          0.20
processVariancePerDay    0.0001
maximumProcessDays       30
maximumInnovationZ       3.0
minimumVarianceLog       1e-6
```

## 14. First observation

With no prior measured state:

```text
meanLogWpm = observation.adjustedLogPerformance
```

```math
P = \max(R, 0.20^2)
```

The deliberately broad initial variance prevents one measurement from producing medium/high ability confidence.

The first bounded recent observation stores:

```text
innovationLog = null
```

because no prior estimate exists.

## 15. Process noise

Before every later observation:

```math
P_{prior} = P_{old} + q \times elapsedDays
```

where:

```text
q = 0.0001
elapsedDays = clamp(actual elapsed UTC days, 0, 30)
```

Process noise increases uncertainty. It does **not** subtract ability or assume skill decay.

Its purpose is to prevent variance from shrinking toward zero forever, which would eventually make the estimator unable to respond to genuine long-term change.

An observation older than `evidence.lastObservedAt` fails with:

```text
OUT_OF_ORDER_ABILITY_OBSERVATION
```

rather than applying negative process time.

## 16. Innovation and robust update

For a new adjusted log observation `Y`:

```math
S = P_{prior} + R
```

```math
r = Y - \mu_{prior}
```

```math
z = r / \sqrt S
```

PL13 does not discard a surprising valid result. It clips influence:

```math
r_c = clamp(r, -3\sqrt S, +3\sqrt S)
```

Kalman gain:

```math
K = \frac{P_{prior}}{P_{prior}+R}
```

Updated mean:

```math
\mu_{new} = \mu_{prior} + K r_c
```

Updated variance:

```math
P_{new} = \max(10^{-6}, (1-K)P_{prior})
```

A low-uncertainty observation therefore moves the same prior more than a noisy observation with identical innovation.

## 17. 95% model interval

Let:

```math
\sigma_A = \sqrt P
```

Then:

```math
Lower = e^{\mu - 1.96\sigma_A}
```

```math
Upper = e^{\mu + 1.96\sigma_A}
```

PL13 calls this a **95% model interval**.

It is not a guaranteed interval around the user's “true typing speed.” The model contains versioned engineering assumptions.

Relative interval width:

```math
RelativeIntervalWidth = \frac{Upper-Lower}{Estimate}
```

## 18. Ability confidence

Ability confidence is not PL11 evidence confidence. It depends directly on latent-model uncertainty as well as repeated evidence.

Canonical levels:

```text
none
low
medium
high
```

### none

```text
observationCount = 0
```

### low

At least one observation exists, but medium/high criteria are not met.

### medium

Requires all:

```text
observationCount >= 3
sessionCount >= 2
upper relative half-width <= 15%
```

where:

```math
H = e^{1.96\sqrt P} - 1
```

### high

Requires all:

```text
observationCount >= 6
sessionCount >= 4
dayCount >= 3
upper relative half-width <= 8%
```

Many noisy observations may therefore still fail high confidence.

## 19. Estimate status

Canonical status:

```text
unmeasured
provisional
established
```

`unmeasured` means no valid observation has been incorporated and all WPM/interval/SRC fields are null.

`provisional` means at least one measurement exists but evidence/uncertainty does not satisfy establishment policy.

`established` requires:

```text
confidenceLevel = medium or high
AND
dayCount >= 2
```

One run cannot produce `established`.

## 20. Smallest reliable change

PL13 exposes an uncertainty-dependent resolution threshold rather than a fixed `+3 WPM` rule.

Approximate log threshold:

```math
SRC_{log} = 1.96\sqrt{2P}
```

Relative threshold:

```math
smallestReliableRelativeChange
= e^{SRC_{log}} - 1
```

WPM threshold:

```math
smallestReliableChangeWpm
= EstimateWpm \times smallestReliableRelativeChange
```

Interpretation:

> Under the current model uncertainty, a difference smaller than this is difficult to distinguish reliably between two similarly uncertain independent estimates.

It is not a biological minimum detectable improvement and PL13 does not declare progress.

## 21. Pure comparison helper

```text
comparePracticeAbilityEstimates(earlier, later, policy)
```

calculates:

```math
\Delta = \mu_{later} - \mu_{earlier}
```

```math
RelativeChange = e^\Delta - 1
```

```math
SE_\Delta = \sqrt{P_{earlier}+P_{later}}
```

```math
z = \Delta / SE_\Delta
```

Model reliability:

```text
abs(z) >= 1.96
```

Practical v1 floor:

```text
abs(relativeChange) >= 0.02
```

Direction is only:

```text
higher
lower
similar
```

The core helper does not return `improved`, `regressed`, `progress`, or `decline`. PL16 owns trajectory interpretation.

## 30. Atomic completed-session integration

The canonical completion input now includes optional:

```text
abilityObservation
```

maximum one per session.

The repository validates an eligible observation before opening the write transaction.

Inside the same transaction as session summary, PL11 skill evidence, review changes, profile mutation, checkpoint deletion, and reconciliation metadata, the repository:

1. verifies session context ownership;
2. checks for an existing completed summary;
3. returns exactly-once idempotent success for the same completed summary;
4. rejects a conflicting duplicate session ID;
5. only then loads the current ability state inside the transaction;
6. creates an unmeasured state if none exists;
7. merges the observation with the pure estimator;
8. validates the merged state and size bound;
9. writes the state in that same transaction.

The state is never read outside the transaction and written later. This prevents lost updates under the repository's existing transactional discipline.

An invalid observation marked eligible aborts the entire completed-session transaction.

An ordinary `not-eligible` assessment carries no observation and the session still commits normally.

The session-summary duplicate check remains the idempotency boundary; retrying the same completed measurement cannot apply ability evidence twice.

## 31. Repository APIs

PL13 adds read APIs:

```text

```

## 32. Context isolation

Every ability state belongs to one immutable PL5 context.

A new context begins unmeasured for every channel. PL13 does not copy a laptop estimate to desktop, QWERTY to QWERTZ, physical to software input, or one locale/context to another.

PL13 also does not enforce cross-channel inequalities such as:

```text
controlled-speed <= burst
```

Observed channel estimates are allowed to vary independently.

## 33. Retention and reset

Ability states are small long-term estimates and are **not** part of ordinary quota-recovery pruning in v1.

They are not discarded merely because they are old.

`resetPracticeData()` clears `abilityStates` together with the other Practice stores and namespaced manifest state.

PL13 does not add arbitrary context deletion or cascade behavior.

## 40. PL16 contract

PL16 may consume ability comparisons, model intervals, SRC, session/day evidence, and retained innovations to distinguish ordinary variation from meaningful long-term change.

PL13 deliberately returns neutral `higher/lower/similar` comparisons rather than declaring improvement.

## 41. PL18 contract

PL18 may introduce empirically matched/equated passage effects. The PL13 estimator is insulated from that future change because passage adjustment lives in the observation builder rather than being scattered through state-update equations.

A future observation model can replace the v1 `0.03 × difficultyIndex` heuristic without redesigning the identity or recursive state schema.

## 42. Final architectural rule

After PL13, Practice Lab must never equate:

```text
LAST SCORE
```

with:

```text
CURRENT ABILITY
```

The measurement chain is:

```text
SESSION PERFORMANCE
        ↓
VALID MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL?
        ↓
TEXT DIFFICULTY ADJUSTMENT
        ↓
OBSERVATION UNCERTAINTY
        ↓
ROBUST LATENT UPDATE
        ↓
ABILITY ESTIMATE
        ↓
95% MODEL INTERVAL
```

One session is an observation of ability, not ability itself.

The correct terminology is:

> **Estimated underlying ability under this Practice channel and context.**

PL13's responsibility is to ensure later Progress, Assessment, Coach, and research systems can operate on an uncertainty-aware latent estimate rather than noisy score chasing.
